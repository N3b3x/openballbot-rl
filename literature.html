Positioning

Where we stand and our possible edge

Where we stand

  • Ballbot RL is thin. The two real works are Salehi (2025, uneven terrain, simulation only, open code, the base of this repo) and asRoBallet (accepted RSS 2026, zero-shot sim-to-real on hardware, no released code found). The rest is small: a DDPG assist, fall-damage arms, iLQG refinement.
  • We overlap both: Perlin terrain and depth or ToF like Salehi; velocity tracking and robustness randomisation like asRoBallet; residual-on-LQR like Zhou 2021 and Hybrid LMC.
  • Our simulation-only 0.75 m/s tracking is inside the range Kugle tested (1 m/s) and below Rezero's claimed 2 to 3.5 m/s, which is itself unverified and conflicting between sources.
  • Our 50 N push recovery has no directly comparable published number; CMU and BallIP report only qualitative push tolerance.

Our possible edge, with the caveats the reports attach

  • Hybrid gated LQR/PPO with a morphology pool: no precedent found. Nearest are Hybrid LMC (residual, one morphology) and mixture-of-experts RL for legged robots. One search pass, so claim cautiously.
  • Parametric design-space study of ballbots with learned controllers: none found; co-design work is legged or generic.
  • ToF multizone sensing plus a height-scan teacher on a ballbot: none found.
  • Quantified traction-limited step climbing (2 to 3 cm): not found for ballbots (Ascento climbs 15 cm, with legs).
  • Reproducibility: asRoBallet has no released code; this repo plus Salehi's Apache-2.0 base gives open baselines.
  • Honest counterpoint: asRoBallet beats LQR strongly under shifted conditions, so our hybrid must beat pure PPO as well as pure LQR. And we have simulation only: no sim-to-real claim.
Honesty

What we could not verify

Conflicts and corrections found while surveying

  • Rezero top speed: 3.5 m/s per IEEE Spectrum, about 2 m/s at 20 degrees per Pham 2024. Conflicting; treat as unverified.
  • BallIP mass: 7.5 kg (IEEE Spectrum) vs 12.5 kg (Robots Guide). Not resolved.
  • The base repo's author is Achkan Salehi (independent researcher); nothing links it to JHU. "Johannes et al." is actually Johannink et al.
  • "Inkwell/Hollis Insect", a CMU "0.6 m/s" figure and a "Pratt" ballbot paper in the original brief could not be matched to any real source and are not cited.
  • Our body-to-ball mass ratio is 23. The survey found no source near that: BallIP is about 2 to 3, the literature roughly 2 to 12.

Not read, or not found

  • Several key PDFs were unreadable to the surveyor: Fankhauser thesis, Pham 2024, Watson 2018, CMU ICRA 2006, Nagarajan 2014, Xiao 2023 and dissertation, Kugle thesis, MBBR 2015. Numbers for them are from abstracts or secondary sources.
  • Rezero mass and ball size, CMU ball mass, Kugle ball diameter and motors, MiaPURE ball and motors: not found.
  • No Disney ballbot paper, no Isaac Sim or Unity ballbot model, no ballbot RL step-climbing paper, no published study of omniwheel roller-count versus fidelity.
  • Torque-to-weight design rules: no source states one; any ratio on this site is derived by us.
  • All 2026 arXiv identifiers and all entries tagged memory need checking. The asRoBallet numbers came through a summariser of the HTML version and should be checked against the PDF.
  • Genesis speed claims are unverified.
