# Shot list: cinematic clips worth rendering (2026-11-08)

Candidates for `experiments/warp_ppo/render_policy.py` (chapters 1 to 3 style) and `experiments/warp_ppo/render_nav.py`
(navigation, needs a trajectory recorded with `eval_nav.py --record K`). Everything below is simulation only, one world per
clip. Severities are given in the units `render_policy.py --severity` expects (metres for steps and drops, degrees for ramps).
Numbers quoted as motivation come from `docs/research/tracking_study_log.md` (S-number given) or `site/data/study.json`.
Nothing here has been rendered; the "point it should make" is the hypothesis the clip is meant to show, and a clip that
shows the opposite is still worth keeping (failures are part of the record).
