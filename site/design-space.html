4. Simulation evidence SIM

Zero-shot LQR on each design, next to the model's prediction

Each design was built as a MuJoCo variant and driven by an LQR that was designed from that design's own model structure, with no tuning on the simulator (scripts/study/eval_design.py). The left block of columns is what the planar model predicts (blue, live); the right block is what simulation did (grey, copied from dz_lqr.csv). They are different kinds of evidence and are never merged into one number.
