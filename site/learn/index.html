How the course works

  • Each lesson: one idea, two diagrams, an interactive figure, a question, key takeaways. Detail is folded away until you open it.
  • You need: high-school physics and a feeling for graphs. Every equation comes with a plain-words reading.
  • The thread: the physics (1 to 3), the controller (4 and 5), sensing (6 and 7), robot design (8), trusting the numbers (9), leaving the simulator (10).
  • Simplified figures: where a figure is a stand-in for the full simulation, its caption says so.
