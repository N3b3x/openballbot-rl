Results at a glance

Five charts, one controller

How goal success rose. Where the learned authority should sit. What still makes the robot fall. When seeing the terrain matters. What carries over to a new robot.

simulation only

TL;DR
  • The recipe: an LQR plus a learned correction with authority 0.25, on a robot with three passive ball arrestors.
  • It tracks speed at 0.85 to 1.00 of the command and reaches a goal across ten terrain kinds 0.73 to 0.74 of the time, 0.45 to 0.46 with pushes and pulls.
  • The arrestors were the step change: without them the same recipe reaches 0.58 to 0.60 and 0.29 to 0.30.
  • What remains: obstacles larger than about a quarter of the ball radius fail at a slow approach. Where they can be avoided, a forward time-of-flight sensor plus a planner takes goal success from 0.09 (blind) to 0.85.
These charts stop at the fixed-authority controller (g18_ret_rs025 and its retrain g26_ret_rs025_mu01). The recommended controller, g39_goalcentre_s0, adds a motion-reference layer, adaptive authority and the goal-centre fix. Its numbers are on the overview and in the results chapters.
