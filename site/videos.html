3 · Terrain

Steps, ramps and stairs: where the limit is

4 · How it got here

Why it used to fall: the ball arrestors

In 95 percent of early falls the body slid off the ball. Three small passive rollers that hold it on lifted goal success from 0.57 to 0.75 with no retraining.

Without rollers: slides off the ball

Flat ground, nothing in the way. The robot rushes the goal, brakes hard and the body comes off the ball.
